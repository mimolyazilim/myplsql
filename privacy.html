Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

This policy covers the MYPLSQL app for macOS, made by Mimol Yazılım Sistemleri Ticaret Ltd. Şti., Türkiye ("Mimol", "we").

In short: we collect no data.

MYPLSQL has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. Nothing you do in the app is sent to Mimol or to any third party.

Your database connections

MYPLSQL connects directly from your Mac to the Oracle databases you choose. Connection details, user names, passwords, SQL statements and query results travel only between your Mac and your database. They never pass through or reach Mimol.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Saved connections (host, service, user name) and preferences.
  • Passwords, only if you choose to save them, encrypted with a key kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Your SQL statement history (last 500 statements) and the files you open or save.

This data stays in the app's own storage on your Mac (or in files you choose) and is removed when you delete the app's data. We cannot access it.

Purchases

The free trial and the MYPLSQL Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. To check whether the trial or purchase is active, the app reads the App Store receipt on your Mac; this information is not sent to Mimol. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Network connections

Besides your databases and Apple's App Store, the app only opens web pages when you ask for them (for example Help → Oracle documentation opens docs.oracle.com in your web browser).

Children

MYPLSQL is a developer tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see Support. Mimol Yazılım — www.mimol.com.tr.
